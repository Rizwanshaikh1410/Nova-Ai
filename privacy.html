Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 03, 2025. This Privacy Policy explains how Nova-AI handles information when users browse and interact with our AI Tool Directory.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nova-AI, one of the world’s leading AI Tool Directories. We provide a free platform where users can explore thousands of AI tools across multiple categories. Nova-AI does not require login, signup, or user accounts to access or use the directory.

Your privacy is important to us. Since our platform does not collect personal data through registration or forms, we collect only minimal, non-identifiable information necessary to improve website performance.

For any questions about this privacy policy, you may contact us at: novaai1410@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Since Nova-AI does not offer account creation, login, or profile systems, we do not collect any personal information such as names, email addresses, passwords, or contact details.

2.1 Personal Information

We do not collect the following information because our platform has no user registration:

  • No names or contact details
  • No email addresses or phone numbers
  • No account credentials (username, password)
  • No profile photos or biographies
  • No direct communications unless voluntarily emailed to us

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We collect minimal, non-identifiable technical information automatically when you browse the website:

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • General location (country/region) based on IP — not exact address
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and click interactions
  • Referrer URLs (from where you came)

This information helps us improve website performance and tool recommendations. None of this data reveals your identity.

3. How We Use Your Information

Because Nova-AI is a free-to-access, no-login tool directory, we use non-identifiable information only for:

  • Improving website performance: Understanding which pages or tools users visit the most.
  • Enhancing user experience: Optimizing speed, search results, and category relevance.
  • Detecting technical issues: Ensuring smooth loading and navigation.
  • Security purposes: Preventing spam, abuse, or harmful activities.

We do not use your information for marketing emails, advertising, or data resale.

4. Sharing Your Information

Since Nova-AI does not collect personal data, we do not sell, trade, or share personal information with third parties.

However, the following non-identifiable data may be shared:

  • Analytics Services: Such as Google Analytics to understand website usage trends.
  • Security Providers: For monitoring spam and cyber-attacks.
  • Business Tools: For improving website hosting and performance.

No personal or sensitive data is ever shared because we never collect it.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Nova-AI may use cookies to:

  • Remember basic browsing preferences
  • Improve page loading performance
  • Understand anonymous usage patterns

You can disable cookies anytime through your browser settings. The website will still function normally.

6. Data Security

We use standard security measures to protect our platform from unauthorized access, malware, and cyber threats. Since we do not store personal data, the risk of private information exposure is extremely low.

7. Data Retention

Nova-AI does not store personal data. Automatically collected anonymous analytics data is retained only for statistical and performance analysis.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Although we do not collect personal data, you still have the right to:

  • Disable cookies
  • Request deletion of analytics data (if identifiable by IP)
  • Request information about collected technical logs
  • Opt out of analytics tracking using browser extensions

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect new technologies, legal requirements, or platform improvements. The updated version will take effect immediately after publishing.
